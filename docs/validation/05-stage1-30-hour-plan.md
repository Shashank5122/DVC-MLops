## 3. The smallest credible measurement design — with the arithmetic shown

Three separated layers. **Never blended into one number.** Each is reported separately, per surface.

### L1 — Controlled API repetitions (the quantitative backbone)

```
Per diagnostic:   12 prompts × 5 runs × 2 windows × 2 API providers = 240 observations
Three diagnostics:                                                     720 observations
Per provider:      360 observations (12 × 5 × 2 × 3 brands)
```

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Providers / surfaces | **Surface A1:** Perplexity **Sonar** via the Agent API. **Surface A2:** **Gemini 3 Flash with Grounding with Google Search** |
| Runs per prompt per window | **5** — identical prompt, stateless, no conversation context |
| Windows | **2**, ≥7 days apart (W1 days 3–7, W2 days 8–12) |
| Founder hours | **≈0.5** (oversight only; the agent executes) |
| Cash | Perplexity: 360 × ≈$0.007 tokens ≈ $2.50 + request fee 360/1,000 × $5 ≈ $1.80 = **$4.30**. Gemini: 360 requests × ~1.5 billable queries ≈ 540 queries → **inside the 5,000 free/month grounding allowance = $0**; tokens 360 × ≈$0.0034 ≈ **$1.22**. Retries/failures ~25% ≈ **$1.40**. **Total ≈ $7, cap $40** |
| Rate sources | Gemini grounding [VERIFIED official, ai.google.dev, Oct 2026]; Perplexity [PARTIALLY VERIFIED, three consistent Oct-2026 sources] |

**What L1 can support:** mention rate and citation rate per prompt with a Wilson 95% interval; within-window
run-to-run agreement (Fleiss' κ, citation-set Jaccard, ordering RBO); window-to-window stability.
**What L1 cannot support:** any lift claim (0.30 → 0.60 needs ≈42 runs per arm; we have 5–10), any consumer-experience
claim, any causal claim.

### L2 — Vendor daily monitoring: **EXCLUDED from this baseline**

Removed because trial registration is NO-GO. If you later approve **S1a**, L2 adds ≈2.5 founder-hours and **$0**
cash (Peec's free 7-day agency pitch projects sit outside the client quota) and would give a third surface for

Two constraints would apply if added: the entry-tier pitch-project allowance is **[NOT FOUND]** (gap 32), so

### L3 — Manual consumer-interface captures (the fidelity anchor)

```
Anchor diagnostic:  10 prompts × 3 runs × 1 window × 1 brand = 30 captures
Co-observation:      2 prospects × 3 matched runs            =  6 captures
                                                          ─────
                                                             36 captures × ~2 min = 72 min ≈ 1.2 h
```

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Surface | Consumer web interface, **logged out, fresh session, desktop, US region, inside one named 6-hour window** |
| Method | Human only. **No programmatic extraction** — provider terms bar it (integrity rules 8, 23) |
| Founder hours | **1.2** (C2 + C3) |
| Cash | $0 |
| Coverage | **One brand only.** Diagnostics 2 and 3 are reported as **API-surface measurement only** |

**What L3 can support:** a documented, side-by-side comparison of API-surface and consumer-surface results for
one brand, reported separately with each surface's own n and interval; the prospect co-observation card.
**What L3 cannot support:** a consumer-surface rate for any brand other than the anchor; a calibrated API↔consumer
mapping; any stability claim (one window, 3 runs ⇒ per-prompt interval ≈±0.44).

### Reported separately, always

| Surface | n per prompt | Brand-level n | 95% interval width (brand-level, indicative) | Reported as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Perplexity Sonar (API) | 5 × 2 windows = 10 | 120 per brand | ≈±0.09 if prompts independent — **they are not**; report with effective-n adjustment | "API surface — Perplexity Sonar" |

## 4. Three different questions — kept strictly separate

| | **Extraction accuracy** | **Measurement reproducibility** | **Commercial validation** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | Does our extractor correctly identify brand mentions and citations in a response? | Do repeated observations of the same prompt agree, and does the signal persist across windows? | Will a buyer pay? |
| Instrument | Adjudication set: human labels vs extractor output → precision, recall, F1 | Fleiss' κ across 5 runs, Jaccard on citation sets, RBO on ordering, window-1 vs window-2 delta, Wilson intervals | T1–T4 evidence hierarchy, three-option choice, first-refused price rung, signed proposal or deposit |
| Stage 1 n | **25 responses, founder-labelled** | 720 API observations + 36 consumer captures | **5 buyer conversations, 3 diagnostics delivered, 1 agency interview, ≥1 priced proposal** |
| What we can claim | Provisional precision/recall with wide intervals | Directional stability with disclosed uncertainty | A commercial signal, **not** a statistical conclusion at n = 5 |
| What we **cannot** claim | **F1 ≥ 0.90 (mentions) / ≥ 0.85 (citations)** — needs ~200 labelled responses. **Inter-annotator κ** — needs a second human annotator (S3 deferred) ⇒ **κ = NOT MEASURED** | Any lift claim (needs ≈42 runs/arm); any consumer-surface trend for non-anchor brands | Any percentage, any market-wide inference, any "4 of 5 prefer X ⇒ kill" automation |
| Consequence | **Automated extraction may not be used for paid client-facing reporting in Stage 1** (A3). Diagnostics are human-verified by you before delivery — which is exactly what Block B's 1.0 h/diagnostic buys | Every deliverable carries n, surface, window and interval, and a "what this cannot tell you" section | The decision at Day 14 rests on **counts, verbatim reasons and commitment tiers**, interpreted with recorded reasoning |
| Precision at n = 25 | If observed precision is 0.90, Wilson 95% ≈ **[0.72, 0.97]** — reportable, wide, honest | — | — |

**Independence:** these three do not trade off. A reproducible measurement with poor extraction is worthless;
accurate extraction of a non-reproducible signal is worthless; and **both can be perfect while nobody pays** —
which is why Block A holds 57% of the hours.

## 6. Limitations of this design — stated before execution, not after

1. **Per-prompt precision is ±0.29 to ±0.33** (5 runs). A rate of 0.4 means "between roughly 0.1 and 0.7".
2. **Brand-level intervals are optimistic** unless the correlated-prompt adjustment is applied; the deliverable
   must show effective n, not 120.
3. **Consumer-surface coverage is one brand, 30 observations, one window.** Diagnostics 2 and 3 are API-only and
   must say so on the cover. No API↔consumer calibration is possible at this n.
4. **No lift, no causality, no placement guarantee** — ever (integrity rule 7).
5. **Extraction accuracy is provisional**: n = 25, founder-labelled, κ NOT MEASURED. Automated extraction is
   **not** approved for paid client-facing reporting.
6. **Segment discrimination is weakened**: 3 diagnostics (2 × S1, 1 × S2) cannot compare S1/S2/S4 on measurement
   evidence. Only the 5 conversations carry segment signal, and at n = 5 that is directional.
7. **Single-week windows** may coincide with news or product events; W2 is the only control.
8. **n = 5 conversations is not statistical evidence.** It is interpreted with recorded reasoning; no automatic
   kill threshold applies (your A4 instruction).
9. **Costs are actuals only after Day 14.** Until then, margin figures remain `[ESTIMATED]`.
